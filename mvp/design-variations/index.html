Catalog overview for Clubhouse Skin. Click a card to open it full size, then pick one.

A · Clarity

White, hairline borders, one navy accent. Stripe/Linear calm. KPI strip, chart beside rankings, your products below.

B · Navy rail

Logo-navy sidebar on a soft blue-grey ground. One big visibility number fused with the chart; white cards.

C · Briefing

No sidebar: top navigation. Opens on a plain-English sentence of what changed, then a wide chart with stats on the side.

D · Catalog shelf

Product photos lead: your six products as a shelf with visibility under each, slim icon rail.

E · Graphite

Dark analyst mode. Same structure as A, built for long sessions and demos on a big screen.
