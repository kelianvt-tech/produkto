Catalog Optimization Agent: five layouts

Same Produkto look, same Clubhouse Skin data, five ways to organise the agent. Each one has every page: Drafts, a draft, Calendar with its detail, Live and all six Setup steps. Use the bar at the top of each to switch pages, versions, and light or dark.

1

Document

A draft reads like the page it will become, with changes tracked in red and green and the agent's questions in the margin.

Best for: reading every word before it goes live.
Open 2

Review queue

A three-pane inbox: folders, the queue, and the page open beside it, before and after side by side. Approve and the next one opens.

Best for: clearing many drafts quickly.
Open 3

Agent feed

A running log of what the agent did today, with the answers it needs from you inline. Setup is a conversation, one question at a time.

Best for: feeling the agent is working for you.
Open 4

Pipeline board

Columns from Needs your facts to Live; open a card and it slides in beside the board. The calendar is a week of columns you drag cards between.

Best for: seeing where everything stands at a glance.
Open 5

By product

Your catalog as the home page: each product with what the agent wrote and which facts are missing. Setup shows a live preview of how pages will read.

Best for: matching the rest of Produkto, which measures by product.
