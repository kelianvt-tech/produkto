Promptwatch helps marketers track & improve their brand visibility in AI responses to drive more high-intent traffic to their website.

Getting started with Promptwatch is as simple as entering your brand’s URL and selecting 5 prompts related to your category. Promptwatch will then start collecting your brand and your competitor’s brand mentions in the AI responses.

### How does Promptwatch collect data?

Promptwatch collects data by scraping the UI interfaces of the LLMs used every day by hundreds of millions of people. This includes ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, Perplexity and others.

### Which LLMs does Promptwatch monitor?

Promptwatch monitors all AI models, ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, Claude, Grok, etc.

### Can Promptwatch track across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all at once?

Yes. Promptwatch tracks your brand across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, AI Overviews, Perplexity and more from a single unified dashboard. Every prompt you monitor runs across all supported models at once, so you can compare your brand’s visibility, citations, and sentiment model-by-model in one place — no need to check each AI engine separately.

### How does Promptwatch help brands get revenue from AI Search?

Promptwatch helps brands generate revenue from AI search by revealing what content AI prefers, tracking when AI agents access your site, and producing optimized content that boosts AI recommendations and drives high-intent traffic.

### Is Promptwatch suitable for agencies?

Yes, Promptwatch is the best solution for agencies managing AI Search for multiple brands. Easily onboard and manage any client and start optimizing for AI Search.

### Can I track my competitors with Promptwatch?

Yes, you can specify any competitor and Promptwatch will show your brand’s visibility compared to theirs.

Promptwatch analyzes which sources AI cites in responses and uses that data to create articles optimized to be referenced more often in AI responses.
