How it works

## Visibility, measured from the answer.

Searchable reads the AI response itself: who appears, where they rank, and which sources shape the recommendation.

01

### Choose the prompts

Buyer questions, category searches, and competitor comparisons.

02

### Run them across AI platforms

The same prompt set is checked daily for comparable results.

03

### Score the answers

Mentions, rank, position, sentiment, citations, and share of voice.

04

### Show what changed

Movement is tied back to the prompt, platform, answer, and source.
