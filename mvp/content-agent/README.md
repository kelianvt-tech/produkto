# Agentic AI Search Optimization

*(Code name: content agent. Files, routes and the switch keep the `content` name; everything a merchant sees says Agentic AI Search Optimization - Kelian, 15 Oct 2026.)*

An agent attached to the Produkto tracker that turns Opportunities into written fixes: product-page changes, FAQs, comparison pages and buying guides. Started 14 October 2026.

**Status:** **phase 1 done, demo-ready** (days 1–15, 14–15 Oct 2026) (14–15 Oct 2026). Day 1: switch, tables, Content tab (Inbox / Calendar / Published). Day 2: `catalog_facts.py` reads each product's public catalog data and product-page markup into one record, every fact tagged with its source. Metafields need store access (phase 3, day 29b). Day 3: `briefs.py` turns an Opportunity into a brief (questions + current results, the merchant's products with their facts, competitors AI named and what their pages say). Only some competitor pages are read so far (cached fetches); reading the rest is a later improvement. Day 4: `briefs.choose_types()` picks what to write by written rules (no AI call) and gives a plain reason for each pick. Day 6: FAQ blocks with FAQPage markup; answers must quote stated facts or are removed. Day 7 (15 Oct): `drafts.comparison()` (the store's products plus up to two competitor products whose page we read; every cell copied from that product's own text or blanked to "Not stated") and `drafts.guide()` (blog guide recommending only the store's products; picks must quote stated facts, paragraphs naming another brand are removed). All fact checks match whole words only ("Yes" no longer matches inside "polyester"). Tried on Clubhouse Skin: ~0.15 cent each. Day 8 (15 Oct): `factcheck.py` checks every sentence of every draft. Numbers not stated in a source are replaced for free; then one helper-model call marks each sentence stated / general / unsupported, and unsupported ones become "[ask merchant: …]" placeholders that also land in the merchant's open questions. General advice is allowed only in guides and comparisons. Over 4 real drafts: ~0.2 cent extra per draft, nothing invented found; once it flagged a true sentence (it errs strict, run to run). Also 15 Oct: when no written rule picks the draft type, the helper model picks and the reason says "chosen by AI". **Day 12 must refuse to export a draft that still holds an "[ask merchant" placeholder.** Day 9 (15 Oct): Inbox with New / Approved / Rejected filters and Approve / Reject / Move back buttons; each card shows the product, open questions and how many sentences the fact check held back. Approve is disabled (and refused by the server) while a draft has "[ask merchant" gaps. Day 10 (15 Oct): `content_review.py`, the draft view (click a card): before/after side by side, a box to answer or leave out each "[ask merchant" gap (answers become stated facts, FAQ markup is rebuilt), the facts that would make the page stronger, and "Edit the text" as plain form fields (no HTML; the description editor strips everything but simple formatting). ## Scope (agreed 14 Oct 2026)
- **v1, draft only (~2–3 weeks):** Opportunity card → brief → draft → review inbox → export file the merchant imports into Shopify. Hidden behind a switch in `config.py`, like Opportunities was.
- **Later, only after a brand pays:** one-click publishing to Shopify (needs write access to the store).
- **Not doing:** crawler logs (Shopify doesn't expose them), auto-publishing without review.

## Rules
- Never invent a product fact. Every spec in a draft must come from the brand's own catalog or page.
- Describe the work, never promise a result. Nobody has proven that fixes move visibility yet.

## Designed AEO articles (19 Oct 2026)

Kelian saw the plain guides on `produkto-test` and rejected them; the approved look is `shopify-test/article-mockup-skincare-2026-10-19.html`. Built the same day, **off unless `PRODUKTO_ARTICLES_V2=1`** (laptop and server both off; when off, guides are written exactly as before).

- `brand_style.py`: reads the store's homepage, one product page and their CSS (free). A small cascade finds the font that actually lands on the product title, description and price (Clubhouse: PS Times headings in capitals, Helvetica Neue text, GT America Mono labels, square buttons); one helper call (~0.1¢) settles roles and colours, but only from fonts and colours on the page. Cached a month in `content_settings['brand_style']`; owner overrides in `brand_style_overrides` always win.
- `niche_examples.py`: from the store's own scans, the brand articles AI cites for its questions (not the store, not `briefs.BIG_SITES`), up to 8 read and measured for free, one helper call (~0.2¢) keeps brands only and writes a playbook (length, block order, headings, product and photo placement, tone). Clubhouse: e.l.f., Rodan + Fields, Meadow & Bark, Deconstruct, CeSqua. Structure only, never their words, facts or photos. Default playbook when a store has no scans.
- `drafts.article()`: the writer returns blocks (dek, at a glance, 4-7 sections with optional step labels, product cards, photos, one table, FAQ, closing banner), ~1,000 words, ~1.7¢ with the fact check and self-review. Product claims stay strict; widely accepted category knowledge and ordinary how-to advice are allowed (fact check verdict "knowledge", articles only); the store's other product pages count as facts; numbers may come from the buyer question ("under £40").
- `article_render.py`: blocks to HTML in the brand's look, scoped under `.pk-a`, with the store's own photos (packshots in cards, lifestyle shots wide, none repeated), prices on buttons, FAQPage markup (gaps excluded), gaps highlighted. On a test store connection it also styles the theme's title.
- `article_learning.py`: **Ask for changes** on the draft view (~0.4-0.7¢): rewrites the blocks, re-checks only changed sentences, may change the look (only to the store's own fonts and colours), and keeps every lasting preference as a lesson. **Hand edits** are compared in the background (~0.01¢) for 0-2 lessons; filling a gap is never a lesson. Lessons go into every future article; Settings › Article look and lessons lists them with Remove, plus the look controls and the niche sources.
- Shopify: **Preview on your store** puts the article up as a hidden draft (customers can't see it) and links to it in the admin; publishing makes that same article visible, with the cover photo, summary and category tag. Refused when the connected Shopify isn't the store's own, like Put live.
- Tested on a copy of the server DB (Clubhouse): article drafted, two change requests applied and learned, one hand edit learned; posted to `produkto-test` and checked in Chrome. 21 tests in `test_articles.py`; all 264 pass in one process.
- **Your articles** (`article_setup.py`, Kelian: "learn this for every single user... very different niches"): a setup step after Your brand. Common questions for every store (readers and their level, what articles help with, length, which blocks, photo style, how hard to sell, products to feature / never use, topics to avoid, byline, expert background, blog categories, claims the industry doesn't allow, a closing disclaimer, articles they love) plus 4-8 questions written for the store's niche by one helper call (~0.25¢), every answer pre-filled from the store's pages; the owner reviews. Tried on three niches: Clubhouse (routine order, skin types, fragrance and sensitivity, sunscreen pairing), Wild One (dogs vs cats, fit, washable materials, walks and night visibility), Onyx Coffee (brew methods, grind, origin and processing, equipment, storage). Answers marked as facts become brand facts; compliance lines join the fact check's no-claims; length, byline and disclaimer go to the writer and the page; never-use products are dropped in code; loved articles lead the niche examples. A store that never opens the step gets it pre-filled once when its first article is written (marked not reviewed).
- Not done: comparison pages still use the old plain layout; the stories hub (all articles as cards) isn't built; photos are the store's own only (no stock or generated yet); no mobile check yet.
