## Live on app.getprodukto.com

**Shopify connection safety**
- Connecting checks the address against the store's own `myshopify_domain` (from its `/meta.json`). A different store is refused at connect and at the Shopify callback.
- A wrong existing connection shows a "Wrong Shopify store" banner, and "Put live" is refused.
- Code: `shopify_connect.expected_shop` and `shopify_connect.mismatch`.

**The agent split in two** (Kelian's names; MEMORY 2026-10-18):
- In the sidebar, Catalog Optimization Agent opens two sub-tabs, each with an icon: **Product page fixes** and **AEO articles**.
- **Product page fixes:** Drafts · Live · Results · Settings. Board: Needs your facts → Ready → Live. No calendar.
- **AEO articles:** Drafts · Calendar · Live · Results · Settings. Board adds Scheduled. The monthly writer button lives here.
- Settings are shared. The lane is in the address (`lane=fixes|articles`), with each user's last lane remembered.
- **Drafts stays the drag-and-drop board** (version 1 restyle): photo, name, what changes, one status, date. The "Review" list+detail layout (`content_board.review()`) is built but unused. Don't switch without asking.

**Monthly AEO article writer** (`monthly_articles.py`)
- On the 1st it writes the plan's article count from **tracked** buyer questions only, ranked by loss × winnability.
- It spreads them over the month's publish slots. They are never approved automatically.
- It says when it runs short of questions.
- Off for stores with no plan limit until the "Write 10 articles for this month now" button is pressed.

**Writing quality** (both agents)
- *Answer first:* question headings, a 30–50 word direct answer, and a banned-AI-phrase list (from the "capsule content" video, taken lightly). Product fixes open by saying what the product is and who it's for.
- *Learns from approved drafts:* the last 3 approved drafts, with the merchant's edits shown, go in as style examples (`examples.py`).
- *Cited pages as research:* the sub-questions the cited pages cover. Big authority sites are labelled "cited for who they are", so their layout isn't copied.
- *One automatic rewrite* when the grader fails a draft, keeping the better-graded version. The fact check runs again on the rewrite.
- *Internal links:* comparison pages link the store's own products and never competitors. A live product fix links to live guides that feature it.
- *Winnability:* each article's "why" panel shows the share of citations from big sites (green/amber/red).

**Monthly self-review** (`agent_review.py`), both agents, on the 1st before the writer:
- Before (14 days) vs. since, per live page, on its buyer questions.
- Verdict: moved / dropped only past daily noise, otherwise no change / too early / can't measure.
- 2–5 lessons fed into next month's drafts.
- Re-fixes for flat or dropped fixes after 30 days.
- A **Results** tab per agent, and MCP `get_agent_results`.
- What can be said on calls: "The agents review their own results every month… every draft also learns from what you approve and edit." **Not** "it keeps getting better".

**Buyer question suggestions** (`question_suggestions.py`)
- Buyer questions now has sub-tabs **Your questions** and **Suggestions** (badge).
- Layout is version 1, made plainer:
  - Now → Suggested.
  - The reason is shown: a dot per answer with the ones naming a product lit, or the product page's words highlighted.
  - Progress bar and in-place Swap / Add / Not for us.
- **Swaps:** questions where AI names products in <15% of answers (it answers with advice), plus the old "Improve buyer question" checks, which moved here from Opportunities.
- **Adds:** ingredient, material, location, competitor, cited page, buyer journey, season, catalog gap.
- Filters: no brand, no product-page copy (compared against the product's own description only), no near-duplicates, no how-to or type-choice questions.
- Rebuilt weekly (~1¢). MCP: `list_question_suggestions` and `decide_question_suggestion`.

**Onboarding questions** (`questions.py`)
- Narrow questions now take one ingredient or material plus a buyer need, instead of copying the page.
- Every set includes at least one problem-led question, with every category covered.
- No advice-style questions (type-choice, set-vs-separate, how-to).

**Pricing page** (getprodukto.com/pricing)
- "MCP access" on all three plans.
- "Monthly self-review of what it put live" on Growth, plus a comparison-table row.
