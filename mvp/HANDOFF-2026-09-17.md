## House rules that are easy to break

- **Product level, not brand level.** The brand card is identity (name, aliases, country, currency) so mentions can be matched — never brand analysis (persona, tone of voice, descriptions).
- **Never invent data.** No fake testimonials, logos, trend points or backfilled zeros. Empty states say "No scans in this period".
- **Questions freeze at the first scan** and are enforced in `db.save_prompts`. Don't add a way around it.
- **No daily grouping.**
- **Percentages, not "X of 25"**, anywhere a user can see.
- Full list in `../MEMORY.md`. Read it before changing anything.
