What this mockup is deciding

  • Score reads as a verdict, not a number. 5/100 in red with "essentially invisible" beside it — the score will be low for almost everyone, so the design has to make a low number land as a finding rather than a bug.
  • Named-in and average-position sit under the score because they answer the first question anyone asks: am I not showing up, or showing up badly?
  • The scan bar is always visible. 5 questions, 25 answers, web search on. It's the difference between a measurement and a marketing claim, and it pre-empts "where does this number come from?"
  • Only your product gets colour. Competitors stay grey throughout, so the eye finds you in every panel without reading a single label.
  • The trend panel is honest on day one — one reading, no invented history. Hit "Preview 8 weeks" to see it populated.
