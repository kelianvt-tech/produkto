## 1. The reality check (read once)

| You asked for | What's safe | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 50–100 connection requests a day | **~50 a day across both accounts** (25 each) | LinkedIn caps each account at roughly 100–200 requests a week. Sales Navigator doesn't raise that. 100 a day needs 4 accounts. |
| 2 posts a day, each account | **1 post a day per account, weekdays** (2 a day for the company) | A second post within ~24h competes with the first and both get less reach. The second "slot" goes into comments instead (section 5). |
| Viral content | **Reach among DTC founders**, not general virality | A post that goes viral with marketers and job seekers books zero demos. 2,000 views from founders beat 200,000 from everyone. |

That gives you about **1,000 founder connections a month** and **~40 posts a month** across both accounts.

## 2. How it turns into customers

```
Connect (no note)  →  they accept  →  message 1: free check offer
        ↓                                        ↓
They see your posts every day      "yes" → run a free Produkto check
        ↓                                        ↓
Post CTA: "comment CHECK"  ─────→  send results → demo → paid plan
```

The same offer as the cold emails: **a free check of how AI picks their products, sent to them.** One offer everywhere, so you learn one thing.

**Sync the channels by niche.** The week's connection requests, posts and cold emails all target the same niche (e.g. coffee & tea one week, pet the next). A founder gets the email, sees a post about AI picks in their category, then gets your connection request. Three touches, one story.

## 3. Who posts what

Two different voices, so the accounts don't repeat each other.

**Kelian — the data person.** What AI actually recommends, and why.
- Real ChatGPT answers in a niche: who got picked, what their product pages say that others don't.
- Building Produkto in public: what you shipped, what a merchant asked for, what you got wrong.
- Cheat sheets: what a product page needs for AI to understand it.

**Gabe — the sales person.** What founders actually say.
- Lessons from calls and demos: objections, what surprised founders, what they didn't know.
- "I showed a founder where AI ranks their products. Here's what they said."
- Founder-to-founder: running sales for an early startup.

## 4. Weekly content calendar (per account)

| Day | Kelian | Gabe |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **AI shelf report:** one buyer question in this week's niche, the real answer, who got picked | Lesson from last week's calls |
| Tue | Cheat sheet (image) | Founder story / objection breakdown |
| Wed | Building in public | Reshare + comment on Kelian's Mon post with his take |
| Thu | **CHECK post:** "comment CHECK and I'll run a free check of your catalog" | Demo moment: what a founder saw for the first time |
| Fri | Myth vs reality (e.g. "SEO ranks you, so AI will too") | Week recap: what we learned |

**Mix over a month:** ~50% teaching (reports, cheat sheets), ~25% stories, ~15% CHECK posts, ~10% direct offer.

### Formats that get reach with this audience
1. **Real answer teardown.** Screenshot of a real ChatGPT answer + "here's why these 3 got picked." Highest value, nobody can fake it. Must be a captured answer with date and prompt saved.
2. **Comparison cheat sheet.** "What Google reads vs what AI reads on a product page." Three columns, same structure in each. These get saved and reshared.
3. **The comment-keyword post.** "Comment CHECK." Drives comments (which drive reach) and every comment is a lead.
4. **The before/after.** A product page before and after the Catalog Optimization Agent, with the facts it added. Describe the work, never promise the result.

### Hooks (first two lines decide everything)
- "I asked ChatGPT for the best cold brew under $20. It named 9 brands. Here's what the top 3 had in common."
- "Your product page says 'premium quality'. AI can't do anything with that."
- "A founder saw where AI ranks their products for the first time today. Their first question surprised me."

## 5. Daily routine (~45 min each)

1. **15 min: comment before you post.** 5–10 real comments on posts by DTC founders and bigger e-commerce creators. Add a point or a question, never "great post".
2. **Post** (scheduled in advance, see section 6).
3. **First 30 min after posting:** reply to every comment.
4. **15 min: inbox.** Reply to accepts and replies. Anyone who says yes or comments CHECK: run the check that day.

This is the one part that can't be automated. The comments are your second daily "post".

### Connection sequence
- **Request:** no note. (Free accounts get very few notes a month, and no-note requests are accepted at similar rates.)
- **Message 1 (on accept):** thanks + the free check offer, two lines.
- **Message 2 (4 days later, if no reply):** one line, same offer.
- Stop there. Two messages, then they just see your posts.
