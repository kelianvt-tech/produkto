## Patterns

**1. Their biggest posts are milestones we can't copy yet.** Peec "$0 → $10M ARR" (4,924), Profound "$180M Series D" (4,201), Peec "$21M Series A" (2,057), Donnelly "$5M ARR in 9 months" (1,020). Post our own milestones the day they're real (first paying client, first named case study), and not before.

**2. The resource roundup is the best repeatable format.** Donnelly's "These are the best 12 free AI guides I could find" got 1,727 reactions, 434 comments and **270 shares**, the most shares in the set. Structure: one-line promise → numbered list (name, one line on what it does, link) → his personal pick → sign-off. Shares are what spread it.

**3. The cheat sheet with "Do this today".** Donnelly's sheet posts are one image plus a long caption: 3–4 numbered blocks, each with a one-line principle, two short "→" lines, and **"Do this today:"** with one concrete action. Ends with a question ("Do you know which of the 3 you're weakest at?"), a sign-off ("Let's keep building, Chris") and the offer in a **P.S.**, not in the body. He posted SEO vs AEO vs GEO on 18 Oct, so don't copy that cut.

**4. "Run this yourself" posts are short and do well.** Lily Ray's "these are ChatGPT fan-out queries in Search Console, use this regex to check your own sites" is 488 characters and got 1,347 reactions. Her best non-milestone posts are all a finding plus a check the reader can run in 2 minutes.

**5. Original data posts work at mid-range.** Ethan Smith's "ChatGPT now has 20% share of search" (466) and Kevin Indig's chart posts (180–220) are their best non-announcement posts. A chart, a finding, then the method in small print at the end.

**6. Relatable mindset posts get the biggest one-off reach in e-commerce.** Chase Dimond's top post (5,248) is a mindset image, not marketing. High reach, low leads. At most one in ten posts.

**7. Format and length.** Image posts do best (1.05× each creator's median), then video (1.00×), carousel (0.91×) and text only (0.90×). Long posts (1,500+ characters) do best (1.13×). Only 2 of 400 captions ask readers to comment a keyword (a keyword may still sit in an image). Donnelly's comments come from the question at the end and the P.S. offer.

## What we take

| Use | From | How we make it e-commerce |
|---|---|---|
| Cheat sheet + "Do this today" | Donnelly | Product pages and stores, not websites in general |
| Resource roundup | Donnelly | Free tools a store owner can use to see how AI sees their products |
| 2-minute self-check | Lily Ray | One question to ask ChatGPT about your own best seller |
| Data + method in small print | Ethan Smith / Kevin Indig | AI Shelf Reports by category (optional) |
| Offer in the P.S. | Donnelly | The free check |
| Relatable mindset | Chase Dimond | Rare, founder-life angle (Gabe's lane) |

**Don't:** copy Donnelly's topics (he's a direct competitor), put numbers in a post that we can't source, or comment in his AEO threads (he sets the frame there).
