Google Shopping = getting your products into Google's product listings, AI Overviews and AI Mode
HOW IT WORKS:
Reads your Merchant Center feed and your product pages
Matches products to the search, filtered by price and stock
PROS:
Huge existing demand
Free listings alongside the ads
CONS:
Crowded with ads and price competition
Messy feed data gets you filtered out
HOW TO RANK HIGHER:
Complete feed: GTIN, price, stock, size, color
Product structured data that matches the feed
Reviews on the product page
WHAT TO TRACK:
Impressions and clicks in Merchant Center
POWER MOVE:
Fix the feed data on your top 10 products before adding anything new

ChatGPT = getting your product named when a shopper asks ChatGPT what to buy
HOW IT WORKS:
Searches the web for the question, reads pages and reviews
Names a few products, often with product cards
PROS:
Shoppers arrive having already decided
Only a few products get named, so a pick stands out
CONS:
No ranking report: you only know if you ask
Answers change from one ask to the next
HOW TO RANK HIGHER:
Answer buyer questions in plain text on the page
Get mentioned in reviews, Reddit and roundups
Don't block OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt
WHAT TO TRACK:
Which of your products it names, for which questions
POWER MOVE:
Ask it your buyer's question, open the winner's page, add what it states that yours doesn't

Perplexity = getting your product named, and your pages cited as a source
HOW IT WORKS:
Searches the web live for every question
Shows the sources it used next to the answer
PROS:
You can see exactly which sources it trusts
Win those sources and you win the answer
CONS:
Smaller audience than Google
Sources shift as new content appears
HOW TO RANK HIGHER:
Get into the articles it already cites
Clear specs it can quote word for word
Don't block PerplexityBot in robots.txt
WHAT TO TRACK:
Which sources it cites when it names a rival
POWER MOVE:
Find the article it cites most for your category and get your product into it
