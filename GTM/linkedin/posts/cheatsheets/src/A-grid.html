AI shopping cheat sheet
4 things an e-commerce brand can control, and what to do today
1
Can AI read your store?
If AI crawlers are blocked, nothing else here matters.
Info hidden in images or pop-ups is invisible to it.
Do this today: open yoursite.com/robots.txt and check it doesn't block OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot.
2
Does your page answer the buyer's question?
Buyers ask with sizes, materials, prices and use cases.
"Premium quality" answers none of them.
Do this today: list the 3 questions buyers ask before buying your best seller. Check the page answers each in plain text.
3
Do other sites talk about it?
AI leans on reviews, Reddit and "best of" roundups.
Your own site is only part of what it reads.
Do this today: search "best [your product type]" on Reddit. Note which brands people mention.
4
Is your catalog where AI shops?
Google and ChatGPT both take product feeds directly.
Not in them? You're relying on luck.
Do this today: check your products are approved in Google Merchant Center.
