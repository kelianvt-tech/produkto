AI Shopping Cheat Sheet

What e-commerce brands can control in 2027.
AI doesn't recommend brands. It recommends products. These are the 4 things that decide whether yours get picked.
1Can AI read your store?
yoursite.com/robots.txt
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Disallow: /  ← AI can't get in
If AI crawlers are blocked, nothing else on this sheet matters.
Product info hidden in images or pop-ups is invisible to it.
Re-check after every theme or app change.
DO THIS TODAYOpen your robots.txt. Check it doesn't block OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot.
2Does your page answer buyers?
BUYER ASKS"running shoe for flat feet, under $150"
YOUR PAGE SAYS"Premium quality. Ultimate comfort."
Buyers ask with sizes, materials, prices and use cases in the question.
"Premium quality" answers none of them.
State the answer the way the buyer phrases the question.
DO THIS TODAYWrite the 3 questions buyers ask before buying your best seller. Check your page answers each in plain text.
3Do other sites talk about you?
WHERE AI LOOKS
Reddit threadsReviews"Best of" roundupsForumsYour site
AI leans on reviews, Reddit threads and "best of" roundups.
Your own site is only part of what it reads.
A product nobody mentions is hard for AI to recommend.
DO THIS TODAYSearch "best [your product type]" on Reddit. Note which brands people mention.
