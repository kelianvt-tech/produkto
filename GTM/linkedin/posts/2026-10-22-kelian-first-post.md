## Caption

AI doesn't recommend brands.

It recommends products.

And most product pages give it nothing to work with.

When a shopper asks ChatGPT "what's the best running shoe for flat feet under $150", it doesn't care how good your Instagram is.

It needs to find a product that fits the question.

Here's what you actually control:

1. Can AI read your store?

→ If AI crawlers are blocked, nothing else on this sheet matters.
→ Product info hidden in images or pop-ups is invisible to it.

Do this today: open yoursite.com/robots.txt and check it doesn't block OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot.

2. Does your page answer the buyer's question?

→ Buyers ask with sizes, materials, prices and use cases in the question.
→ "Premium quality" answers none of them.

Do this today: write down the 3 questions a buyer asks before buying your best seller. Check your page answers each one in plain text.

3. Do other sites talk about your product?

→ AI leans on reviews, Reddit threads and "best of" roundups.
→ Your own site is only part of what it reads.

Do this today: search "best [your product type]" on Reddit and see which brands people mention.

4. Is your catalog where AI shops?

→ Google and ChatGPT both take product feeds directly.
→ If you're not in them, you're relying on the AI finding you by chance.

Do this today: check your products are approved in Google Merchant Center.

Most brands I talk to are strong on one of these and haven't looked at the other three.

Which one is yours?
