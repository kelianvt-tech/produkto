## Carried over from Kelian's rules (sales-relevant, as of 2026-10-06)

- **For cold-call lead pulls, "N leads" means N phone numbers.** About 30% of leads have a mobile, so pull ~3.5× the target and report the phone count first. *(2026-10-06)*
- **Every lead pull is one niche, B2C e-commerce only, with the owner's name and own phone (+1 E.164).** First live calls hit a B2B pigment supplier ("we do B2B, not B2C"). *(2026-10-05)*
- **Every lead pull ends in two CSVs: with-phone (to call) and no-phone (to email/DM).** *(2026-09-29)*
- **Close (the CRM/dialer) defaults phone numbers to Spain.** Import as +1 and check prefixes before dialing. *(2026-10-05)*
- **Never use an unverified email address.** Early sends had 15–22% hard bounces, which damages every inbox. Keep bounces under 2%. *(2026-08-12)*
- **Never send cold email from getprodukto.com.** Cold email goes out from tryprodukto.com, useprodukto.com and produktohq.com. *(2026-10-02)*
- **The first 100 cold emails (Aug 2026) got zero replies** using a "you don't rank, your competitor does" hook. Don't reuse that angle. *(2026-09-29)*
