# Calling Script — Produkto

*Written 2026-09-30. Replaces sections 4–6 of `GTM/COLD-CALLING-PLAN.md` as the thing you read from on calls. One goal: a 20-minute demo booked before you hang up. Don't sell Produkto on the phone.*

Each lead's line is ready in the call sheet (`leads/call-sheet.csv`) and in Close (the **Opener** field). You fill in **[Name]**; the products come from the sheet.

---

## 1. Opener (first 10 seconds)

*Structure from Marcos (Senecta), 2026-10-01: name and title, a competitor in the first sentence, then ask permission. The competitor is one ChatGPT actually recommended (see the call sheet), never a claimed customer.*

> "Hey [Name], I'm Kelian, the founder of Produkto. I'm calling because I've been looking at which [product type] ChatGPT recommends, and the [Competitor product] from [Competitor brand] keeps coming up. Can I take 15 seconds to explain why I'm calling?"

**Never say "we're working with a competitor of yours".** Produkto has no customers, so it would be false, and "which one?" ends the call. Naming a competitor the AI recommends gives the same pull and is true.

**Where the products come from:** the call sheet. The competitor is the product ChatGPT recommended when asked a buyer question about their product type. **Check the `Flag` column before you dial:**

- **ok** → use the line above.
- **THEIR product recommended** → "…I've been looking at which [product type] ChatGPT recommends, and your [Their product] keeps coming up. Can I take 15 seconds…?"
- **anything else** → use the version without names:

> "Hey [Name], I'm Kelian, the founder of Produkto. I'm calling because I've been looking at which [product type] ChatGPT recommends, and it only names two or three. Can I take 15 seconds to explain why I'm calling?"

- **"Sure / go ahead"** → section 2.
- **"I'm busy"** → "Totally. When's better, later today or tomorrow morning?" Log `CB` with the time.
- **"Who is this?" / suspicious** → "Kelian, I run a small company called Produkto. We measure how brands show up when shoppers ask ChatGPT what to buy." → section 3.

## Two rules (Marcos, 2026-10-01)

- **No negative pitch.** Never say "you're not showing up" or "you're not ranking well". People hate having their weak spots pointed out by a stranger. Talk about what they'll *see*, not what they're getting wrong. On the demo, let the dashboard show the gaps; they'll draw the conclusion themselves.
- **Never knock competitors**, whether rival brands or other AI tools (Glara, Profound and so on). It makes you sound untrustworthy. Naming a competitor brand the AI recommends is fine; criticising anyone isn't.

## 2. What we do, and why they should care (two sentences, then ask)

> "We track which of your products ChatGPT, Google's AI and Perplexity recommend every day, across your whole catalog. It covers every product you sell, not a handful, and shows you exactly which products get picked for each question."

**What we do** in one sentence, then **why it's better** in one. Then ask if they care, and stop talking:

> "Could you help me understand how relevant this would be for you guys?"

**Don't sound like you're reading.** Pause, say "mm", restart a sentence now and then. Once they hear a script, the call is over.

## 3. Short conversation (let them talk)

Whatever they say, pick **one** follow-up. Their answers are research, write them down.

| They say | You ask |
|---|---|
| "No, never looked." | "Most brands haven't. Do you see any traffic coming from ChatGPT in Shopify or Google Analytics yet?" |
| "Yes, we've checked." | "What did you find? Were your products in there?" |
| "We use a tool already." | "Which one? And does it cover the whole catalog or a set number of products?" |
| "Our agency handles it." | "Makes sense. Are they measuring AI answers, or Google rankings? They're different questions." |

## 4. Ask for the demo

> "Here's what I'd suggest. In 20 minutes on Zoom I'll show you the dashboard with real data: which of your products get named, for which questions, and how it changes day by day. If it's not useful, you've lost 20 minutes. Does this week or next work better?"

Book a **30-minute** slot in Calendly for a 20-minute demo, so there's room if they have questions.

- **Yes** → book it in Calendly while they're on the line. Confirm their email out loud. Send the invite before you hang up: "You should have it now, can you see it?"
- **Hesitant** → "What would you need to see for it to be worth 20 minutes?" Answer that, ask again once.
- **Still no** → "Fair enough. Can I send you a two-line email so you have it?" Get the address.

## 5. Objections

Answer once, then go back to the question in section 4. Don't argue.

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Not interested." | "No problem. So I learn something: is it that AI isn't a channel for you yet, or just the timing?" *(log the answer)* |
| "Send me an email." | "I'll definitely send you one. It's just hard to show this in an email; it's a live dashboard. Would you be open to 20 minutes this week or next?" If still no: "What's the one thing that would make it worth opening?" Send within 10 minutes. |
| "AI is tiny for us." | "It is for most brands right now. That's why it's cheap to check where you stand. The demo tells you whether it matters for you yet." |
| "How much?" | "Plans start at $99 a month. I'd rather show you the data first so you can judge if it's worth it." |
| "Can you fix it / make us show up?" | **Write this down word for word.** "That's exactly what I'd like to talk through on the demo." |
| "Can you guarantee more sales?" | "No, and nobody can honestly promise that. What you get is the measurement, so you can see whether changes move anything." |
| "How did you get my number?" | "From a business database of e-commerce founders. If you'd rather I don't call again, I'll take you off the list right now." *(if yes → do-not-call in tracker)* |
| "Is this AI / a robot?" | "No, it's me, Kelian. Calling from Barcelona, hence the accent." |

**Never say** "nobody else does this", "we'll get you recommended", or how many questions a scan asks.

## 6. Gatekeeper

> "Hi, it's Kelian. Could you put me through to [Name]? It's about how [Brand]'s products show up when shoppers ask ChatGPT for recommendations."

- **"What's it regarding?"** → same line, calm. Don't pitch the gatekeeper.
- **"They're not available."** → "When's the best time to catch them?" + "Could I get their direct email?" Log `GK` + callback.

## 7. Voicemail (under 20 seconds)

> "Hi [Name], Kelian from Produkto. We show brands which of their products ChatGPT recommends when shoppers ask, and which competitors it picks instead. I'll send you a short email. Speak soon."

Leave a voicemail on attempt 1 and 3 only. Not every dial.

## 8. Follow-up email (same hour)

> **Subject:** [Brand] in ChatGPT answers
>
> Hi [Name], I just tried calling. Produkto shows which of your products ChatGPT recommends when shoppers ask, and which competitor products it picks instead.
>
> If you'd like to see it for [Brand], here's 20 minutes: [Calendly]
>
> Kelian

## 9. Questions to ask on the demo

Ask near the end, after they've seen the dashboard. Write the answers down word for word.

- **Perception:** "Would it matter to you *how* AI describes your products, not just whether it mentions them? For example, if it gets a price or an ingredient wrong?" *(Testing whether to build product perception. Build it only if two or three brands light up.)*
- **Remediation:** "If you saw a product missing here, what would you do about it?" *(Tells you whether they want the tool or want it fixed for them.)*

## 10. After every call

Log the outcome in `tracker.csv` (`NA` `VM` `GK` `NI` `CB` `DEMO` `BAD`). Every real conversation, even a "no", gets a note in `research/<date>-call-<brand>.md`: what they said, the objection, anything about tools or agencies they use.
