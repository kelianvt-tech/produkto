# Demo cheat sheet

Keep this open on every call. Built from Karol's feedback (20 Oct mock demo, Loom) and the 5Strands demo (21 Oct).

## Before the call
- [ ] Ask: **"Will anyone else be involved in the decision?"** If yes, get them on this call or book a second one.
- [ ] Decide who does what. **Gabe:** frame, questions, close. **Kelian:** 5-minute screen.
- [ ] Dashboard open and loaded on their brand. Know the number, who beats them, and one fix.
- [ ] Discount amount and Stripe link ready.

## 1. Frame (2 min)
"We track what AI says when people ask for products like yours, then fix your product pages so it recommends you. I'll ask a few questions first, then show you only what matters to you. Sound good?"

## 2. Questions, no screen share (10–12 min, they talk 70%)
- What made you take the call?
- Where do customers come from today? What's getting harder?
- Have you tried anything for AI or search? What happened?
- What do your buyers actually type?
- If AI keeps recommending others, what happens in a year?
- Why now and not in six months?

After every answer: **"What makes you say that?" / "How long has that been going on?" / "What did that cost you?"** Write down their exact words.

## 3. Label it (1 min)
"So: [their words]. And it matters now because [their words]. Fair?" Don't move on until they say yes.

## 4. Show it (5 min max)
1. **Their number as a gap:** "AI names someone else in 9 of 10 answers."
2. **Who wins instead.** "Are these who you lose to?"
3. **One fix** on their own product page. "Anything here you'd never say?"

## 5. Close
"Normally $297 a month. Start today and the first month is $[X]." Ask for the card on the call.

## If they stall
- **"I need to think about it"** → "Sure. What specifically?"
- **"I need to ask my partner/CEO"** → "What will they want to know?" Then book a call with them before hanging up.
- **"Skeptical of AI content"** → "The agent drafts, you approve. Nothing goes live without you."

## Never say
- "Not bad" / "pretty good" about their results
- "Starter scan" / "not accurate yet" / "still in demo"
- "Our past users" / "most of our users" / "it trains itself every day"
- "Nobody else does this"
- Promises of more sales or a visibility number
- How many questions or runs a scan uses

## Always end with
A booked next date, or a card. Never just "we'll send an email."
