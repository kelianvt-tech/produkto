# Mock demo with Karol Lang (KLL Digital), 20 Oct 2026

Source: Loom "Meet - produkto <>KLL Digital: Demo - 20 October 2026", 51 min (https://www.loom.com/share/07b0806729604eca8d033df93c36a515). Karol runs a Meta-ads agency (average $3.5–4k/month retainer) with a sales team that closes 20–25 deals a month. Kelian demoed Produkto (Clubhouse Skin), then Karol gave feedback. Gabe was on the call too.

## Karol's verdict
"What you've done here will not close, and it has nothing to do with the product." Kelian pitched for about 8 minutes, then asked discovery questions at the end. Karol zoned out partway through.

## Feedback
- **Talk ratio 70/30, with the prospect talking most.** Start with discovery, not the pitch.
- **Frame first:** why should they listen to you? With no case studies yet, you have to be creative.
- **Discovery is at least 40% of the call, and it's where the sale is made.** Find the problem, say it out loud, and make it urgent. People book a call because something is hurting their business. Questions: "What made you book the call?", "Why do you feel you need to look at AI search now?", "How long has that been a problem?", "Have you tried anything?", "Why not?" Keep digging past vague answers. Structure: Hormozi's CLOSER (Clarify, Label, Overview past pain, Sell the vacation, Explain away concerns, Reinforce).
- **Pitch tailored to what they said,** as a three-step plan ("remember you told me X, this is how we solve it"). Make it interactive, and **5 minutes max**. Sell the outcome, how likely they are to get it, and why it matters. Explain mechanics only if they're technical.
- **Charge something, even $100:** "whatever they pay, they pay attention". Suggested framing: normal price, but if they start today, the first month is just the usage cost (~$500). Tie the discount to deciding on the call.
- **Objections:** anything other than paying is an objection, and all of them are about trust. Dig into "I need to think about it".
- You don't have to guarantee sales or visibility. Sell solving the problem. Early goal: get lots of clients in for data, not quick money.
- Later: a pre-call video so the live demo can stay short. Record every call (Fathom) and review it.
- **Cold calling:** use the same discovery questions on the call, keep it conversational, and lower their guard. Commission-only dialers once there's a paid product. He called the current cold emails "atrocious".
- He disputed Kelian's "no one else does this for e-commerce" line.
