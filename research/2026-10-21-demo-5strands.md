# Demo: 5Strands (Ethan Steed, COO), 21 Oct 2026

Source: phone recording of the call (WhatsApp video, 19.5 min), transcribed with Whisper on 22 Oct. The transcript is machine-made, so a few words are probably misheard (e.g. "Clever Fox"). **The recording starts with Gabe's pitch already under way, so any discovery before that isn't captured.** Kelian and Gabe on our side. Prep: `2026-10-21-demo-prep-5strands.md`.

## What Ethan told us (his words, roughly)
- Their AI search "should be bad"; organic is "okay".
- **His partner, the CEO, "hates leaning on AI".** They use it, but don't want to rely on it.
- **"Not fully my decision."** He'll run it by the CEO.
- They just hired two teams (Meta, Amazon); an internal tech team covers Shopify; data is "a little locked up".
- **Past pain: an old agency (fired) targeted the wrong words.** They wrote "allergy" when the product is an intolerance test, and most buyers don't know the difference.
- The searches he wants to win are symptom questions: "my dog's chewing his feet", "best at-home test".
- Asked whether we can show who is searching (demographics). Answer: no, the AI platforms don't share it.
- Asked: subscription or percentage? Can they pick the buyer questions? Has Claude MCP users on his team.
- Had never heard of "Test My Food Sensitivity".

## What we showed
Visibility 10% average (14% today), the competitors, per-product visibility (3%), one answer where 5Strands ranked first, buyer question suggestions, Opportunities and the agent (described, not opened), then pricing: Starter, Growth $297 with the article packs thrown in free.

## Outcome
No card, no next date. Ethan will talk to the CEO. We promised a recap email with pricing.

## Against Karol's feedback (Loom, 20 Oct)
| Karol's rule | What happened |
|---|---|
| Prospect talks 70% | Reversed: Kelian and Gabe did most of the talking. |
| Discovery 40%, where the sale is made | Mostly skipped (at least in the recording). His real pains came out by accident and were never dug into. |
| Label the problem in his words | Never done. The "allergy vs intolerance" pain and the symptom searches were the obvious label. |
| Make it urgent | Undercut: "results are not bad at all", "actually pretty good", "you're mentioned almost every time first". |
| Pitch 5 min, tied to what he said | About 10 min of features (MCP, suggestions, onboarding, tiers), mostly not tied to his words. |
| Card on the call | Not asked. The decision maker (the CEO) wasn't on the call, and nobody found out before or during. |
| "I need to think about it" → dig | "Let me run it by him" was accepted without asking what the CEO would object to. |

## Things said that we shouldn't say
- "It is our starter scan, so it's not that accurate yet." This undermines our own numbers.
- "Of course you're mentioned here, it's a very specific question." Same problem.
- "Trained from a bunch of data from our past users" / "for our users it's been a lot easier" / "most of the users go on Growth": we have no past users. That's false, and it'll come out.
- "It trains itself every single day... humanizing itself", "AI can detect AI", "looks at your Instagram": not what the product does, or unverified.
- "We do this with every person we get on demos": fine as a tactic, but keep it consistent with the real plan (Growth has 0 articles; packs cost $10 each).
- The "don't select the 20 a month" checkout instruction confused the offer. Send one link with the right plan instead.

## What to do differently next time
1. Confirm who decides **before** the call ("Will anyone else be involved in the decision?"), and get the CEO on it.
2. Open on his pain: "You mentioned the old agency wrote 'allergy' when it's intolerance. How much did that cost you?" Then show the dashboard **only** for symptom questions like "dog chewing paws".
3. Frame the numbers as the gap: 10% means AI names someone else in 9 of 10 answers. Never "not bad".
4. Pre-handle the sceptical CEO: the agent drafts, a human approves, nothing goes live unchecked.
5. Close for a next step even without a card: "Can we do 15 min with you and the CEO on Thursday?"
