How To Master AI Shopping

The shift that decides which of your products get recommended.
WHAT IS AI SHOPPING?
More shoppers skip the search results and ask an AI assistant what to buy. It reads the web, then names a few products. If yours isn't one of them, the shopper never sees it.
BEFORE AISearch, open 10 tabs, compare, pick one. Your ad and your ranking get a look.
»»
WITH AIAsk once, get 3 names, buy one. Only the 3 named products get a look.
Think of it this way: Google gave you a page of chances. AI gives you three.
HOW TO SET UP YOUR BEST SELLER
1Write down the 3 questions buyers ask before buying it
2Ask ChatGPT and Perplexity each question, the way a buyer would
3Note every product they name, and the sources they cite
4Open the winners' pages: list the facts they state that yours doesn't
5Add those facts to your page in plain text, plus a short FAQ
6Ask the same questions again in a few weeks and compare
EXAMPLE: 1 PRODUCT PAGE, ONE AFTERNOON
1Buyer asks: "best dog harness for a puppy that pulls, under $40"
2AI names: three harnesses. Yours isn't one
3Their pages state: front clip, sizes by chest in inches, "for pullers"
4Yours states: "comfy, durable, stylish"
5You add: clip position, size chart, who it's for, an FAQ
Key rule: if a buyer would put it in the question, put it on the page.
ANATOMY OF A PAGE AI CAN RECOMMEND
Every product page AI can match has three parts. Miss one and it guesses, or skips you.
