1Make sure AI can actually read your store.
If OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Googlebot can't read your product pages, nothing below this layer exists. It's the one that breaks most, and the cheapest to fix.
OAI-SearchBotPerplexityBotGooglebotrobots.txtRaw HTMLText, Not ImagesVariant Pages
2Describe each product the same way everywhere.
Your product page, Google Merchant Center, Amazon and your feed need to agree. Three different titles and prices, and you've built three weak products.
Product TitlesMerchant CenterProduct FeedAmazon ListingSchema MarkupGTINPrice Match
3Answer the buyer's question on the page.
Shoppers ask with a size, a material, a price and a use case in the question. "Premium quality" answers none of them. State each one in plain text.
Size & FitMaterialsUse CaseWho It's ForWho It's Not ForSpecs TableProduct FAQs
4Get mentioned where the models already look.
AI leans on reviews, Reddit threads, "best of" roundups and YouTube reviews. This layer isn't built on your store, it's built where buyers already talk.
RedditReview Sites"Best Of" ListsYouTube ReviewsGift GuidesCreatorsForums
5Get named as the pick (not listed beneath it).
Being the recommendation takes proof a model can lift: reviews that name a use case, clear comparisons, real numbers. You earn this layer last.
Use-Case ReviewsComparison PagesReview VolumeAwardsReal NumbersNamed Use CasesTrust Signals
6Give the shopper who already decided a reason to buy.
Shoppers who arrive from AI have usually decided already. Land them on a page that confirms the choice, not a homepage and a pop-up.
Clear PriceStock VisibleShipping TimeReturnsReviews Up TopSize GuideFast Checkout
