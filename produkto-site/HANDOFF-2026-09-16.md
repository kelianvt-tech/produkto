## What Produkto is
Pre-launch tool for Shopify brands: pick one product, Produkto asks ChatGPT the questions buyers ask about it, then shows a visibility score, share of voice, which competitor products ChatGPT picks instead, and what's missing from the product page. It has zero customers and the scan itself isn't built yet, so the site sells early access.

## Design rules
- Navy sections use `--navy-deep: #050f22`. Approved navy sections: `#statement`, `#answer`, `#request`, footer. Everything else stays on the light background. Ask before making any other section dark.
- No gradients or glows on the navy sections.
- Accent colours are soft tints of existing site colours. Keep it consistent and restrained.
- Only change the exact section I ask about. Don't redesign neighbouring sections, nav, buttons, FAQ or footer unless asked.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any animation.

## Content rules (important)
- Never state how many buyer questions or runs a scan uses (no "five questions", "five times", "25 answers"). Say "your buyer questions, each asked several times"; use percentages instead of "X of 25".
- Keep M&O Living and any furniture-audit data completely off the site.
- No invented testimonials or customer claims. Produkto has no customers yet.
- ChatGPT answers shown on the page must be real captures (record the prompt, answer and date), never made up. Don't pair them with invented "your product" names.
- Don't claim "nobody else does this"; competitors exist.
