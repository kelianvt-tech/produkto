Produkto feels like a terminal at night: dark navy, monospaced, quiet, precise. It doesn't shout. It types out a fact, cuts hard, and moves on. The message underneath everything: your catalog is now read by machines, and we take care of that for you.

Calm control

  • A night-shift operator, not a hype machine. Composed, certain, slightly cold.

  • Mono type, dot-matrix marks, blinking cursors. It looks like it was built for how AI reads.

    • Proof over promise
