What it does

The main features, in plain words.

Whole-catalog, product-level measurement

Paste a Shopify store URL. Produkto reads the catalog and measures every product, not a capped number.

Competitors found automatically

Every competing product AI names in the answers shows up, with share of voice. Nothing to configure.

Citations

The sites AI cites for each question, and whether they mention the brand.

Opportunities

A ranked list of what to fix: questions the brand loses, product details competitors state, sites that don't mention it.

Catalog Optimization Agent

On Growth: drafts product page fixes from the store's own facts. The brand approves each one before it goes live.

MCP access

Connect Produkto to Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor and ask about your visibility from there.
