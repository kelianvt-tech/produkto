Step 1: Write your buyer questions

The questions decide the result, so spend most of your time here. Start with one product or category and write a few questions for it.

Leave out your brand name

"Is Brand X's serum good?" tells you how AI describes you, not whether it recommends you.

Include the need

"Best moisturizer for oily skin in winter" is how people ask. "Best moisturizer" isn't.

Write prices with a currency symbol

"Under $40", not "under 40". Without the symbol, AI can misread the number.

Cover different moments

A "best X for Y" question, a comparison between two options, and a "what should I buy for…" question.

Step 2: Ask in a clean session

Assistants can remember earlier conversations and adjust to you. To see what a new shopper sees, start clean every time.

ChatGPT

Use a Temporary Chat or turn off memory. Check that the answer used web search: it shows sources.

Perplexity

Use an incognito thread. Every answer lists its sources.

Google

Search in a private window and note whether an AI Overview appears. For many searches it doesn't, and that's a result too.

Location matters

Answers depend on where the shopper is. If your customers are in the US, check from the US, or the answer can name different products and stores.

Step 4: Repeat

AI answers are generated fresh each time, so the same question can name different products an hour later.

Ask each question several times and come back on another day before drawing a conclusion. Stop when the pattern stops changing: if your product is named in roughly the same share of answers each time you check, you have a reliable reading.

Why one check isn't enough

A product named in one answer out of many is in a very different position from one named in most of them. A single screenshot can't tell those apart.

Where the manual check stops working

The manual check is the right way to start. It gets hard to keep up past a handful of products.

More products

Every product needs its own questions, asked on every assistant.

Change over time

A trend needs the same questions asked the same way, every week.

Did a fix work?

Telling whether a page update made a difference needs a before and an after on the same questions.

Frequently asked questions

Does ChatGPT give everyone the same answer?

No. Answers vary between runs, and memory, location and whether web search is used all change them. That is why you check in a clean session and repeat each question.

Should I include my brand name in the question?

Not for this check. A question with your brand name tells you how AI describes you. A question without it tells you whether AI recommends you to someone who hasn't heard of you yet.

How many times should I ask each question?

Several times, on more than one day. Stop when the pattern stops changing: if your product is named in roughly the same share of answers each time, you have a reliable reading.

What if AI recommends my product but describes it wrongly?

Note it and check the sites it cites. Wrong details often come from a page AI read, such as an old listing or a retailer page, and fixing that source is the place to start.
