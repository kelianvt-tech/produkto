How we compared them

We make Produkto, one of the tools on this list. To keep this useful, every fact about another tool comes from that tool's own website, and we say what each one is best at.

Products, not just the brand

Most AI visibility tools track whether your brand is mentioned. A store needs to know which products are recommended, for which questions.

How much of the catalog

Some tools track a set number of products or prompts per plan. For a store with hundreds of products, that limit decides what you can see.

Which AI assistants

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google build different shortlists, so coverage matters. In our own data they rarely agreed.

Measurement, fixes, or both

Some tools measure. Some audit or rewrite pages. Some do both. Decide which job you're hiring for.

How to choose

Five questions narrow the list quickly.

  1. Do you need product-level answers?

    If you sell products, "is our brand mentioned" isn't enough. Look for tools that show which products are recommended for which questions.

  2. How many products do you need covered?

    Count the products that matter, then check each plan's limit on products or prompts.

  3. Which assistants do your buyers use?

    ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google give different answers. Check which ones each plan covers, not just the top tier.

  4. Do you want measurement, fixes, or both?

    A tracker tells you where you stand. An audit or agent changes your pages. Some tools do both.

  5. Are you already paying for an SEO suite?

    If your team lives in Semrush or Ahrefs, their add-ons are the least friction. They are built around brands and prompts rather than catalogs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI visibility tool for a Shopify store?

It depends on the job. To see which of your products AI recommends across the whole catalog, look at product-level tools like Produkto, Glara or Sixthshop. To track brand mentions inside an SEO suite you already use, Semrush or Ahrefs. For a large enterprise program, Profound.

What's the difference between brand tracking and product tracking?

Brand tracking tells you whether AI mentions your company. Product tracking tells you which of your products it recommends, for which questions, and which competing products it picks instead. A store can be mentioned often and still have most of its catalog left out.

How much do AI visibility tools cost?

On the pricing pages we checked in October 2026, entry plans ranged from free to about $250 a month, with enterprise plans priced through a demo. Limits on prompts, products and AI models vary a lot between plans.

Do these tools improve my visibility?

They measure it, and some audit or rewrite pages. Which changes reliably move AI recommendations is still an open question, so measure before and after any change on the same questions.
