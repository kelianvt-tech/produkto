Guide design: five directions

The same article, “What is AI shopping visibility?”, with identical facts, in five different designs. Pick one (or parts of several) and it becomes the template for every guide.

1. JournalA printed long-read: Newsreader and Source Serif on warm paper, a drop cap, a pull quote, and sourced numbers set as margin notes beside the text they support.2. Research reportIBM Plex on a strict grid with a sticky contents rail and reading progress. Numbers become a real chart and a sourced list; tables and figures are labelled like a report.3. PosterHuge Geist type, a full-height navy opening and orange accents. Stats become sentences, SEO vs AI is a three-column spread, steps are big rows.4. Answer anatomyThe page explains itself: an annotated AI answer up top, then each section is a buyer question with a one-line answer first and detail beside it. Instrument Serif headings, teal accent.5. Field guideWarm and human: Fraunces on cream, highlighted key sentences, sourced numbers in a margin column, SEO vs AI as a side-by-side spread.
