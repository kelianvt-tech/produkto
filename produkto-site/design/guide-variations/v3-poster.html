What is AI shopping visibility?

AI shopping visibility is how often AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity name your products when a shopper asks what to buy.

The short version

It is measured per product, on the questions your buyers actually ask, and compared against the competing products AI recommends instead.

Shoppers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google what to buy. They get back an answer that names a few products, not ten blue links.

A product that isn't named in that answer is never considered. There is no page two.

Answers change from one run to the next, so it is measured across many answers and tracked over time, not checked once.

It is also called AEO, GEO, AI search visibility or LLM visibility. “Shopping” narrows it to the thing a store sells: products.

How it differs from SEO

SEO asks whether a page is relevant to a search. AI shopping visibility asks whether a product gets named when a shopper describes what they need.

Strong SEO still helps. Assistants with web search read the same pages search engines rank. But a page can rank well and still leave out the details an assistant needs to match the product to a specific need, like who it suits, what it’s made of or what it can’t do.

How AI assistants choose products

With web search on, an assistant reads a handful of pages and writes its answer from them. What those pages state decides which products it can recommend.

  1. The shopper asks with a need

    “A waterproof jacket for hiking in heavy rain, under $250.” One question carries a category, a use, a condition and a budget.

  2. The assistant searches

    It runs one or more web searches built from the question. Google describes running several related searches at once for AI Mode.

  3. It reads sources

    Product pages, editorial roundups, retailer listings, reviews and forum threads.

  4. It matches products to the need

    Products whose pages confirm the conditions (waterproof rating, use, price) are easy to recommend. Vague pages give it nothing to work with.

  5. It names the few it can justify

    Usually three to five products, often with links to the sources it used.

“What’s a good waterproof jacket for hiking in heavy rain, under $250?”

  1. 1Ridgeline Storm Shell 3-layer, 20K waterproof rating, pit zips
  2. 2Northpass Rain Jacket Light, packs into its pocket
  3. 3Your jacket Named third: its page states the rating and seam taping
  4. —Your other jacket Not named: its page doesn’t say how waterproof it is

An illustrative answer. The products are invented.

How to measure it

Ask your buyer questions several times, on more than one assistant, and track four numbers per product over time.

Visibility

The share of answers that name your product.

Average position

Where your product appears when it’s named. First beats fourth.

Share of voice

Your mentions as a share of all product mentions in those answers.

Citations

The sites AI cites, and whether they mention you.

An assistant can recommend one of your products for one need and never mention the rest of your catalog. A brand-level number hides that.

What you can work on

Most of it is product data: making each page state the facts a shopper’s question depends on, and being mentioned on the sites AI reads.

Who it’s for and what it’s for

“For hiking in heavy rain” can be matched to a question. “For any adventure” can’t.

The specs buyers filter on

Materials or ingredients, sizes, weight, ratings, compatibility. Written in text, not only in images.

Stated limits

What it isn’t suited for. It helps AI recommend the product for the right question.

Answers on the page

The questions customers ask support, answered in a sentence or two on the product page.

Complete structured data

Product markup with price, availability and reviews, matching what the page says.

Mentions where AI looks

The roundups, retailers and forums that keep showing up in the citations for your questions.

Which of these moves visibility most is still an open question, for us and for everyone working on it. Measure first, change one thing, and measure again on the same questions.

Questions people ask

Is AI shopping visibility the same as AEO or GEO?

Close. AEO and GEO cover any content an AI might quote or cite. AI shopping visibility is the part that matters to an online store: whether your products get recommended.

Does ranking on Google mean AI will recommend my products?

Not necessarily. Assistants read pages that rank well, but they recommend specific products for specific needs. A product page that doesn’t state what the product is for can rank and still be left out of the answer.

Why does my product show up in one answer but not the next?

AI answers are generated fresh each time, so they vary. That is why visibility is measured as a share of many answers, not from a single check.
