# Launch film v3: voiceover script (draft 2)

Male voice, calm and confident. The narration runs the whole film and says more than the screen shows. About 135 words for 48.6 s, a natural pace for a launch voiceover.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.5 | "In today's AI world" | Shopping has changed. People don't scroll through ten blue links anymore. They ask AI what to buy. |
| 4.5–8.0 | 51% stat | More than half of U.S. consumers have already used AI to help them shop. |
| 8.0–12.5 | The question is typed, ChatGPT answers | So when someone asks for the best leash for their golden retriever, AI reads the web, compares products, and names its top five. |
| 12.5–18.0 | Wild One "Not in the answer" | If your product isn't on that list, you're not even in the running. And most brands have no idea whether they show up or not. |
| 18.0–21.5 | "Introducing" + P spin + agent name | Introducing Produkto, and its Catalog Optimization Agent. |
| 21.5–25.0 | Hundreds of thousands of data points | It's built on hundreds of thousands of data points about what AI actually recommends, and why. |
| 25.0–33.5 | Agent fixes the wildone.com page | It finds the product pages AI is skipping, works out what's missing, and rewrites them using only facts from your own store. Nothing made up. You approve every change, and it publishes straight to Shopify. |
| 33.5–38.5 | Checked every day | Then it checks, every single day, where each of your products stands in AI answers, |
| 38.5–43.0 | AI logos | across ChatGPT, Google, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and more. |
| 43.0–48.6 | End card: the onboarding's spinning particle P, getprodukto.com under it | Produkto. See where your products stand. Book a demo at getprodukto.com. |

Facts checked: the 51% matches NielsenIQ; "publishes straight to Shopify" matches the agent's Shopify publishing (approval first, with undo); the engines named match the logos on screen (Kelian added Claude and "and more" on 2026-10-20). Nothing promises more recommendations.

If the voice runs longer than a scene, I slow that scene down instead of rushing the voice. The film may grow by a few seconds.
